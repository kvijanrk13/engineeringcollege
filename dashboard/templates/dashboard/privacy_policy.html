ENGINEERINGCOLLEGEPROJECTS provides access to the Engineering College department portal for academic, faculty, student, certificate, report, and document workflows.

Information We Process

The portal may process information entered by authorized users, including:

  • Student profile, academic, contact, certificate, and project details.
  • Faculty profile, academic, professional, certificate, and document details.
  • Uploaded files such as photos, certificates, reports, and generated PDFs.
  • Login/session information needed to keep users signed in.
  • Google account email and profile details when Gmail sign-in is used.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the college department portal, authenticate users, manage academic records, generate reports/PDFs, and support department administration.

Storage And Third-Party Services

The application may use hosted database, file storage, Cloudinary, Google OAuth, and Render hosting services to provide the portal. These services process data only for application operation, authentication, hosting, and storage.

Data Access

Access to portal data should be limited to authorized college staff, faculty, and students. Users should not upload data they are not authorized to manage.

Data Retention

Academic and administrative records are retained as needed for college operations, reporting, compliance, and record maintenance. Authorized administrators may update or remove records as required.

Contact

For privacy or data access questions, contact the department administrator through the college support channel.
