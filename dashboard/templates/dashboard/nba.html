Criterion 1: Vision, Mission and Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

The program must have a documented vision, mission and PEOs. PEOs must be consistent with the mission of the institution and must be reviewed periodically with stakeholder (employers, alumni, faculty, students) involvement. Evidence: documented PEOs, stakeholder feedback, review records.

Criterion 2: Program Curriculum and Teaching-Learning Processes

The curriculum must be designed to achieve POs/PSOs. Teaching-learning processes must be effective, with innovative pedagogies, industry interaction, and continuous evaluation. Evidence: syllabus, lesson plans, industry MoUs, teaching records.

Criterion 3: Course Outcomes and Program Outcomes

Course Outcomes (COs) and Program Outcomes (POs) must be defined, mapped, and assessed. Attainment levels must be computed and used for continuous improvement. Evidence: CO-PO mapping, attainment reports, action taken.

Criterion 4: Students' Performance

Student performance in terms of results, placements, higher studies, entrepreneurship and competitive exams must be tracked. Evidence: result analysis, placement records, alumni data.

Criterion 5: Faculty Information and Contributions

Faculty must be qualified (Ph.D. preferred), with adequate numbers. Contributions in teaching, research, publications, consultancy and FDPs must be documented. Evidence: faculty profiles, publications list, FDP records.

Criterion 6: Facilities and Technical Support

Adequate infrastructure: classrooms, laboratories, library, computing facilities, internet, and technical support. Evidence: lab inventories, library holdings, infrastructure audit.

Criterion 7: Continuous Improvement

Evidence of continuous improvement through feedback, assessment, and action plans. Quality assurance processes must be in place. Evidence: IQAC records, feedback analysis, action taken reports.

Criterion 8: First Year Academics

First-year (basic sciences/engineering) academics must be well structured with adequate faculty and facilities. Evidence: first-year curriculum, faculty, lab records.

Criterion 9: Student Support Systems

Counselling, mentoring, scholarships, and extracurricular support for students. Evidence: mentoring records, scholarship data, student activity logs.

Criterion 1: Vision, Mission and PEOs

Same as Tier-1 but evaluated for affiliated programs following university curriculum. PEOs aligned with institutional mission.

Criterion 2: Program Curriculum and Teaching-Learning

Curriculum delivery and teaching-learning processes assessed; must meet university syllabus with effective pedagogy.

Criterion 3: Course Outcomes and Program Outcomes

CO-PO mapping and attainment; continuous improvement based on assessment.

Criterion 4: Students' Performance

Results, placements, higher studies tracked.

Criterion 5: Faculty Information and Contributions

Faculty qualification and contributions documented.

Criterion 6: Facilities and Technical Support

Adequate labs, library, computing facilities.

Criterion 7: Continuous Improvement

Feedback and action plans for quality improvement.

Criterion 8: First Year Academics

First-year academics structure and resources.

Criterion 9: Student Support Systems

Counselling, mentoring, scholarships.
