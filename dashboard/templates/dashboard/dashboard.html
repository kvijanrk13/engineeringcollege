Academic Framework

Vision, outcomes, and long-term graduate goals

This section organizes the department’s PSOs, PEOs, and POs into a more readable format than the previous stacked layout.

Curriculum Direction

Program Specific Outcomes

The department prepares students to grow professionally and learn emerging computing paradigms with confidence and adaptability.

  1. Advance professionally through organized training or self-learning in areas related to Information Technology throughout their career.
  2. Learn emerging computing paradigms for research and innovation.

Program Educational Objectives

These objectives reflect the broader growth expected from graduates beyond classroom performance alone.

  1. Take up professional responsibilities and communicate effectively with diversified teams.
  2. Support sustainable societal development while adhering to ethics and values.
  3. Practice lifelong learning and produce innovative solutions for complex problems.

PO1: Engineering Knowledge

Apply mathematics, natural science, computing, engineering fundamentals, and specialization knowledge to solve complex problems.

PO2: Problem Analysis

Identify, formulate, review literature, and analyze complex engineering problems to reach substantiated conclusions.

PO3: Design and Development of Solutions

Design creative solutions and system components or processes that meet specified technical needs.

PO4: Investigations of Complex Problems

Use research-based knowledge including experimentation, modelling, data analysis, and interpretation.

PO5: Engineering Tool Usage

Create, select, and apply suitable resources, methods, and modern IT engineering tools for modelling and prediction.

PO6: The Engineer and the World

Evaluate societal, environmental, legal, health, safety, and sustainability aspects of engineering solutions.

PO7: Ethics

Apply ethical principles, professional responsibility, human values, diversity, inclusion, and legal awareness.

PO8: Team Work

Function effectively as an individual, team member, or leader in multidisciplinary and diverse environments.

PO9: Communication

Communicate effectively and inclusively with professional communities and society through reports, presentations, and dialogue.

PO10: Project Management and Finance

Apply engineering, management, and financial principles for effective decisions in project environments.

PO11: Life-Long Learning

Recognize the need for independent and lifelong learning in response to technological change and future challenges.
