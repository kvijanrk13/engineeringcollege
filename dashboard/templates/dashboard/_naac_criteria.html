What NAAC assesses

Seven criteria, one institution, one cycle

NAAC scores the institution as a whole against seven criteria. Every criterion carries key indicators, and every key indicator is broken into metrics: qualitative metrics that the peer team reads and judges on site, and quantitative metrics that NAAC verifies as data before the visit. Open the category that matches your institution, then open any criterion for its focus, what to demonstrate, the evidence file to prepare and the team that should own it.
