# Week 1 - Problem Statement

## II. E-TICKETING – ONLINE RESERVATION SYSTEM

An Online Reservation System (ORS) is a project that manages ticket booking electronically according to passenger needs. In public transportation systems such as buses, trains, and airlines, the passenger is the central user. People depend on these services for work, education, family visits, and other day-to-day travel.

Passengers often find it difficult to obtain tickets and vacant seats, especially during peak hours, holidays, and festival seasons. They may have to visit a reservation counter repeatedly, wait in long queues, or choose a more expensive private transport service. A computerized reservation system is therefore needed to help passengers search available services and reserve tickets in advance for a more comfortable and predictable journey.

### ➢ Existing System

In a traditional railway reservation process, passengers visit a booking counter or contact an enquiry office to obtain information about trains, schedules, fares, and available seats. Ticket availability and reservation records may be maintained through separate counters or manual registers, so updated information is not always immediately available to the passenger.

The existing process consumes time, creates queues, increases paperwork, and places an additional burden on booking-counter staff. Manual entry can also result in duplicate records, calculation errors, delayed seat updates, and difficulty retrieving previous booking information. Passengers cannot conveniently compare services, confirm a reservation, or check its status from home.

### ➢ Proposed System

ETORS (E-Ticketing Online Reservation System) is a web-based railway reservation system that manages the principal reservation activities in one place. A passenger can search trains by source, destination, and journey date; view departure and arrival times, fares, and seat availability; enter passenger details; choose a travel class; and complete a dummy payment to confirm the reservation.

After confirmation, ETORS generates a unique 10-digit PNR and assigns seats automatically. The passenger can securely check the reservation using the PNR and registered mobile number, cancel an eligible booking, select demonstration train insurance, and optionally arrange a BOOKMYCAB transfer from the destination station. Booking requires verified Gmail authentication, while administrators maintain station, train, fare, capacity, and reservation data.

The proposed system has the following advantages:

1. Passengers can search and reserve railway tickets online without waiting at a booking counter.
2. Departure time, arrival time, journey duration, fare, and current seat availability are displayed together.
3. Verified Gmail authentication and server-side validation protect the booking workflow.
4. Dummy UPI, card, and net-banking options demonstrate electronic payment without transferring real money.
5. A unique PNR and automatic seat assignment are provided immediately after confirmation.
6. PNR details are protected using the registered mobile number instead of exposing passenger information publicly.
7. Confirmed reservations can be cancelled, and the released seats return to availability.
8. BOOKMYCAB can be selected for a scheduled destination-station transfer.
9. Centralized digital records reduce paperwork, repetitive calculations, and counter-staff workload.
10. The responsive interface can be used from desktop and mobile web browsers.
