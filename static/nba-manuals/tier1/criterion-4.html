4.2.

Success Rate of the Students in the Stipulated Period of the Program (15)

Success Rate (SR) = (No. of students who graduated from the program in the

stipulated course duration) /(No. of students admitted in the 1st year of that batch and

those actually admitted in the 2nd year via lateral entry, plus the number of students

admitted through multiple entry (if any) and separate division if applicable, minus the

number of students who exited through multiple entry (if any).

Average SR = Mean of SR for the past three batches.

SR Points = 1.5 * Average SR/10.
